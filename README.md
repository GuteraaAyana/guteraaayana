## 🚀 Featured projects

### 🌸 [Scentbook](https://scentbook.online): Letterboxd for fragrance
*TypeScript · Next.js · React · PostgreSQL · Tailwind · Docker · AWS*

[![Live site](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-scentbook.online-f2b33d?style=flat-square)](https://scentbook.online)
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-scentbook-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/GuteraaAyana/scentbook)

<a href="https://scentbook.online"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GuteraaAyana/scentbook/main/docs/screenshots/home.jpg" alt="Scentbook's home page: a search box above a row of fragrance posters" width="100%" /></a>

A full-stack app for people who love fragrance: keep a collection, log every wear (the weather fills itself in), rank what you've tried into your own score out of 10, review, and follow other people's taste. I designed, built and deployed it end to end.

- Built and deployed a full-stack "Letterboxd for fragrances" where users **rank, review, and log wears** of fragrances, with GitHub/Google sign-in, follows, and an activity feed.
- Designed **comparison-based ranking** that places each fragrance by binary search (**≤7 questions for 100 items**) and converts list position into a 0–10 score.
- Modeled a **21-table PostgreSQL schema** with versioned migrations, and imported catalog data from a third-party API with deduplication and name matching.
- Raised mobile Lighthouse performance on the browse page **from 83 to 93** by fixing image loading priority and halving page weight (2.0 to 1.1 MB).
- Deployed on **AWS (EC2, RDS, ECR)** with Docker, automatic HTTPS, and health-checked rollbacks; scored **93–99** Lighthouse performance and **100** accessibility.

### ⚽ [Temporal Dynamics of Goal Scoring in Soccer](https://github.com/GuteraaAyana/amr-soccer)
*Python · Pandas · NumPy · NetworkX · Matplotlib · SLURM*

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-amr--soccer-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/GuteraaAyana/amr-soccer)
[![arXiv](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2501.18606-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18606)

**Co-first author** on research at Northeastern's **Network Science Institute**. We used Hudl StatsBomb event data from **3,433 professional matches across 21 leagues** and compared real goal timing with a null model of **6M simulated matches**.

- Goal-scoring probability **rises as a match goes on**, with far fewer goals than expected early in each half (χ² ≈ 288.62, p ≈ 3.7 × 10⁻²¹).
- Scoring is **bursty**: the team that just scored is more likely to score again soon than chance predicts.
- Extended the work with **event networks and PageRank** to find which sequences of play lead to goals.
- Built reproducible data pipelines on SLURM for the large-scale runs.
